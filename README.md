# 🏍️ Bike Market Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the bike market using Python and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). The objective is to identify pricing patterns, brand-wise performance, engine capacity trends, and mileage relationships to generate meaningful business insights.

The analysis covers **528 bike records across 56 brands**, helping understand different aspects of the bike market through data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze bike prices across different brands.
- Identify brands with the highest and lowest average prices.
- Understand the relationship between engine capacity and bike prices.
- Explore the relationship between mileage and pricing.
- Identify important market trends through data visualization.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python

## ❓ Business Questions

The project addresses 10 business questions to understand:

- Which brands have the highest and lowest average prices?
- What is the overall bike price distribution?
- How does engine capacity relate to bike prices?
- How does mileage relate to bike prices?
- What patterns can be identified across different bike brands?

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Ducati has the highest average bike price among the analyzed brands.
- Yo has the lowest average bike price.
- Engine capacity and price show a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.77.
- Mileage and price show a negative correlation of approximately -0.46.
- The average engine capacity in the dataset is approximately 527.08 cc.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Premium brands can focus on customers looking for high-performance bikes.
- Manufacturers can use pricing comparisons to improve their market positioning.
- Bike companies can study the relationship between engine capacity and pricing when planning product categories.
- Mileage and affordability can be considered when targeting budget-conscious customers.
